# IT onboarding checklist

## Before day one
Accounts are created 3 business days before the start date. Equipment ships 5 business days before the start date to the address on file.

## Day one
Sign in with the temporary password from your manager, enrol in MFA with Microsoft Authenticator, set a new password and install Secure Connect VPN from the Software Portal.

## First week
Join the Teams channels for your team, request access to the SharePoint sites you need and complete the security awareness training within 7 days.

## Problems
Open a ticket with category general and mention that you are a new starter; these are prioritised.

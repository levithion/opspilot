# Printing and scanning

## Secure print
Print jobs are held in the Secure-Print queue until you tap your badge at any printer. Jobs are deleted after 24 hours if not released.

## Adding a printer
Install the Secure-Print queue from the Software Portal under Printing. Do not add printers by IP address.

## Scan to email
Choose Scan to Me at the printer and the scan arrives in your work mailbox within a minute.

## Printer faults
Open a ticket with category hardware and give the printer label shown on the front, for example PRN-3F-02.

# Laptops and hardware

## Standard hardware
New employees receive a managed laptop (MacBook Pro 14 for engineering, Dell Latitude 7440 for other roles), a docking station and a headset. Laptops are refreshed every 36 months.

## Requesting or replacing hardware
Open a ticket with category hardware. Broken laptops get a loaner within 1 business day. Peripherals such as monitors, keyboards and mice are ordered from the hardware catalogue with manager approval.

## Lost or stolen device
Report a lost or stolen device to it-security within 1 hour. IT will remotely lock and wipe it. Do not wait to see whether it turns up. For theft also file a police report and add the reference to the ticket.

## Returning equipment
Leavers must return all equipment within 5 business days of their last day using the prepaid courier box.

# Email and calendar

## Mailbox limits
Each mailbox is 100 GB and each message can be up to 35 MB. Large files should be shared with a OneDrive link.

## Shared mailboxes
Shared mailboxes such as support@opspilot.example are requested by the mailbox owner through a ticket with category access. They appear automatically in Outlook within 60 minutes.

## Out of office
Set automatic replies in Outlook under File > Automatic Replies. Replies to external senders are enabled by default.

## Calendar and meeting rooms
Meeting rooms are bookable as resources in Outlook. A room is released automatically if nobody checks in within 15 minutes.

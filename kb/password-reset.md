# Password policy and reset

## Self-service reset
Use the self-service portal at passwords.opspilot.example. Verify with Microsoft Authenticator and choose a new password. This is the fastest route and needs no IT involvement.

## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, cannot reuse your last 12 passwords and expire every 180 days. A passphrase of four random words is recommended. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff; IT will never ask for it.

## Account lockout
Five failed sign-in attempts lock the account. It unlocks automatically after 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, use the self-service portal to unlock it.

## When self-service is not possible
If you have lost access to your MFA method as well, IT can perform an administrator password reset after verifying your identity. This is an approval-gated action: a password reset request is created and an IT administrator must approve it before the temporary password is issued.

# Software licences

## Standard software
Every employee receives Microsoft 365 E3 (Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, SharePoint). Engineers also receive a JetBrains All Products licence on request. Design tools such as Figma Professional are assigned to design and product roles.

## Requesting a licence
Open a ticket with category licensing and include the product, the business reason and your cost centre. Requests above 200 USD per year per seat need your manager's approval. Standard requests are fulfilled within 3 business days.

## Licence ownership
Each licence has an owner, which is the user the seat is assigned to, plus a cost centre that pays for it. To find who owns a licence or when it renews, ask for an access and licence lookup. Team leads see the licences of their team.

## Reclaiming licences
Seats unused for 60 days are reclaimed automatically and returned to the pool. You receive a warning email 7 days before reclamation.

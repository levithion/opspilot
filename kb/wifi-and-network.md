# Wi-Fi and office network

## Wireless networks
Employees use the Corp-Secure network, which authenticates with your work account (802.1X) and never needs a shared password. Visitors use Corp-Guest. The guest password changes every Monday and is displayed at reception.

## Wired network
Docking stations connect to the wired network automatically once the laptop is enrolled. Only managed devices are allowed on the wired network.

## Slow or dropping Wi-Fi
Forget the Corp-Secure network and reconnect, then move closer to an access point. If many people on the same floor are affected, open a ticket with category network and mention the floor and the time.

## Personal devices
Personal phones may join Corp-Guest only. They cannot reach internal systems without the managed company portal app.

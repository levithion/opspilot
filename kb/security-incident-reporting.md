# Security incident reporting

## Phishing
Use the Report Phish button in Outlook. Do not forward the message. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, change your password immediately through the self-service portal and tell it-security.

## What counts as an incident
Lost devices, suspected malware, unexpected MFA prompts, leaked credentials, data sent to the wrong recipient and any suspicious access to company systems.

## How to report
Post in the it-security channel or open a ticket with category security and priority high. For anything urgent call the on-call number listed on the intranet.

## Response times
Security incidents are handled at priority urgent: first response within 15 minutes during business hours and within 1 hour outside them.

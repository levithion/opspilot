# Data retention and privacy

## What is stored
Helpdesk tickets are kept for 24 months. Chat transcripts with the assistant are kept for 90 days. Audit records of actions taken by the assistant are kept for 7 years and contain only pseudonymous user identifiers, never message text.

## Personal data handling
Emails, phone numbers and identity numbers are redacted before text is sent to a language model or written to logs. Only the minimum data needed for the request is used and it is used only for support purposes.

## Your rights
You can ask to see or delete your personal data. Send the request to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@opspilot.example. Requests are completed within 30 days.

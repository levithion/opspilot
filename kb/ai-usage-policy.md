# Generative AI usage policy

## Approved tools
Approved tools are OpsPilot, Microsoft Copilot with the work account and the company Claude workspace. Using any other AI tool with company data is shadow AI and is not allowed.

## What you must not share
Never paste customer data, credentials, source code marked restricted, or personal data into a tool that is not approved. Personal data must be redacted before it is sent to a model.

## Budgets
Each team has a monthly AI budget. Teams get a warning at 80 percent of their budget and AI calls are stopped at 100 percent until the budget is raised by IT. Team leads can see their team's spend report.

## Requesting a new AI tool
Submit a ticket with category security. New tools need a security and privacy review, which takes about 10 business days, and must be registered so that spend can be attributed to a team.

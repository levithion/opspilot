# Helpdesk support hours and SLAs

## Support hours
The helpdesk is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC. Outside those hours only urgent incidents are handled by the on-call engineer.

## Priorities and targets
- Urgent: first response within 1 hour, resolution within 4 hours.
- High: first response within 4 hours, resolution within 1 business day.
- Medium: first response within 1 business day, resolution within 3 business days.
- Low: first response within 3 business days, resolution within 10 business days.

## Choosing a priority
Urgent is for outages and security incidents affecting many people. High is for a single person who cannot work. Medium is for degraded service with a workaround. Low is for requests and questions.

## Escalation
If a ticket breaches its response target, reply to the ticket with the word "escalate" or message the it-helpdesk channel.

# Finance team software purchase approval

## Approval limits
Software purchases up to 1,000 USD per year need the finance controller. Purchases above 1,000 USD need the CFO and a procurement review.

## Budget codes
Use cost centre FIN-200 for team tools and FIN-210 for audit tooling. Purchases without a cost centre are rejected by accounts payable.

## Audit tooling
Auditors get read-only access to the ledger for the duration of the audit only, and access is removed within 1 business day after sign-off.

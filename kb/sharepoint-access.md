# SharePoint and Teams site access

## How to request access
Open the site and choose "Request access". The request goes to the site owner, who normally responds within 2 business days. If the owner is unavailable, open a ticket with category access and name the site and the access level you need (member, visitor or owner).

## Access levels
Visitors can read, members can edit and owners manage membership. Access is granted to named people, not shared accounts.

## Confidential libraries
Libraries marked Confidential also require your line manager's approval. Access to them is reviewed every quarter and removed if it is no longer needed.

## External sharing
External sharing is disabled by default. Sharing with a customer or partner needs a security review; open a ticket with category security. Anonymous "anyone" links are not allowed.

## Checking what you can access
You can ask OpsPilot to look up your current access grants, which lists the SharePoint sites, repositories and VPN groups attached to your account.

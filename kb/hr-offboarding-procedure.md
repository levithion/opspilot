# HR offboarding procedure

## Notice and access
HR files the leaver request at least 5 business days before the last day. Account access is disabled at 18:00 on the last day. In cases of dismissal, access is disabled immediately.

## Mailbox and data
The mailbox is converted to a shared mailbox for 90 days and the manager gets access. OneDrive content is kept for 30 days and then deleted.

## Equipment
Equipment must be returned within 5 business days. HR confirms the return before the final payment is released.
